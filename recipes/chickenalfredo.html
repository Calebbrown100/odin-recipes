Chicken Alfredo Recipe

Back to Caleb Recipes

Description

Chicken Alfredo is a delicious pasta dish made with grilled chicken, creamy Alfredo sauce, and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of chicken breast
  • 1 pound of fettuccine noodles
  • 1 cup of heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/2 cup of grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the fettuccine noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, melt the butter and add the garlic.
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the parmesan cheese and stir until melted.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Grill the chicken breast until cooked through.
  8. Toss the cooked noodles with the Alfredo sauce.
  9. Slice the grilled chicken and serve over the pasta.
