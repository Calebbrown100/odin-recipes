Lasagna Recipe

Back to Caleb Recipes

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of ground beef
  • 1 jar of marinara sauce
  • 1 package of lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups of ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups of parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups of mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer the lasagna noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
